Those pages are linked from the corresponding profiles or map viewers. Do not treat the local copy as a grant of additional rights; verify official reuse terms before redistribution.

Park-specific photos were not locally available for several national parks. Those pages show an explicit image-source gap instead of substituting an unrelated or watermarked photograph.
